✓ By operators, for operators · Est. 2026

We run the machine desk.

Pack Guys Automation is a Los Angeles reseller of white-label cannabis production machines — vape cart fill & cap, grind, weigh, label — run by operators who got tired of being on the wrong side of the importer conversation.

Every machine seller we dealt with made the same boring transaction feel like a gamble: "request a quote," wait two days, get a number nobody else could see, and hope the unit off the boat matched the stock photo. So we built the opposite — the supplier with the boringly-perfect paperwork.

Posted prices on every model's spec sheet. Each unit serialized, its serial dot-peened into the frame, and its factory-acceptance test filmed with that serial in frame so you can watch yours run before it ships. The wear parts go on a shelf in LA before the crate does.

We don't build these in the USA, and we don't stamp them CE or UL when they don't carry it. Our factories build them; we badge, serialize, test-record, and stock the wear parts here. We'd rather tell you that than pretend — trust compounds when a supplier shows the work instead of claiming "premium."
