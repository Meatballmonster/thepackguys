The pre-launch packaging checklist.

Most packaging compliance failures aren't exotic — they're a missing PDF, the wrong state warning, or a stockout two weeks before a drop. This is the checklist we run before any pre-roll packaging order goes out. Work top to bottom; don't start a production run with an open box unchecked. Informational, not legal advice — confirm specifics with your state regulator.

1. Lock the spec

  • ☐ Size chosen against your cone — 116mm for king-size is the default. Size guide →
  • ☐ Closure confirmed — a hinged squeeze-below-cap pop-top that stays child-resistant on every open-and-close cycle (cap stays attached, can't be lost).
  • ☐ Color/finish decided — and an opaque option available if your state requires it at retail.
  • ☐ Sample ordered and physically checked — cone fits, cap seats, label band sits flat. $14.99 sample case →

2. Get the CR documentation

  • ☐ Lab CR certificate PDF for your exact closure — names the design, the 16 CFR § 1700.20 protocol, the lab, the date, the batch.
  • ☐ Not a generic marketing cert — if there's no batch traceability, walk. What a real cert looks like →
  • ☐ Cert is on file with the licensee and producible on regulator request.
  • ☐ Supplier turned it around in 24 hours — stalling is a red flag.

3. Source the state label

  • ☐ Correct state warning copy — pulled verbatim from the regulator, not paraphrased. Requirements by state →
  • ☐ Cannabis symbol for the state present on the retail label.
  • ☐ THC content + batch/lot fields on the label (and any state tracking number — e.g. Metrc in Oklahoma).
  • ☐ No child-appeal art — no cartoons or fruit imagery (hard rule in MA and NY, good practice everywhere).

4. Confirm the physical safeguards

  • ☐ Tamper-evident seal applied at the point of sale.
  • ☐ Opaque at retail where required — or a B2B clear tube plus a retailer-applied opaque overlay where that's allowed (OK, NJ, FL).
  • ☐ Resealable / lifetime CR verified if you sell into CA, CO, or MA (must stay CR every open-and-close cycle).

5. Plan the reorder before you launch

  • ☐ Lead time known — days from a domestic stock holder, weeks-to-months from overseas. Don't launch on a supplier you can't reorder same-week.
  • ☐ Reorder trigger set — a par level that fires a reorder before you're empty.
  • ☐ Terms in place — Net-30 or upfront, agreed before order #1, not negotiated mid-stockout.
  • ☐ Second state queued — same tube, swap the label. Full procurement playbook →

Print this page or work it on screen. Every box checked means the order won't bounce at the dispensary door or the regulator's desk.
