✓ Before you ask

Procurement questions.

Are you a registered California vendor?
The Pack Guys LLC is a California LLC in good standing with an active Seller's Permit (255-928544). Our federal SAM.gov registration is in review and our CA Small Business certification is in progress — both are noted honestly above, not claimed before they're issued.
What do you supply?
Everyday operating commodities: janitorial and sanitation, office supplies, PPE and safety, LED lighting, textiles and linens, facility storage, hand tools, and comfort/agricultural heating. We source from vetted manufacturers rather than warehousing everything ourselves.
Do the products carry the required certifications?
Product-level marks — UL, FCC, ANSI, TB117 flammability, food-grade — are the manufacturer's responsibility. We collect those documents from the supplier and pass them through with every quote, so your file is complete on delivery.
Do you sell surveillance cameras, networking gear, or medical supplies?
No. We deliberately stay in zero-restriction commodity lanes — no NDAA §889-covered electronics (Hikvision, Dahua, Huawei, ZTE), no Chinese drones, and no FDA-regulated medical or exam devices. It keeps procurement clean for both of us.
How do I request a quote?
Send your line item, a product spec, or a solicitation number to sales@thepackguys.com. We respond with itemized pricing and a posted lead time — no quote-gate, no runaround.
Can you meet small-business or local preferences?
Our CA Small Business certification (a 5% bid preference) is in progress. We're set up to serve California state, county, city, and school-district buyers, and we can register with local vendor systems on request.
