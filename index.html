✓ Before you ask

Quick answers.

What machines do you sell?
Cannabis production machines — the lead is a vape-cart fill & cap line, and we bundle grinding, weighing, and labeling around it. Cone fill is available on request, matched to your cone.
Can I actually watch it run before it ships?
Yes. Every unit gets a filmed factory-acceptance test with its serial in frame — your machine, running, before the crate closes. You get the film.
Do you certify these to UL or CE?
No — and we won't pretend to. These are resold machines that are tested and serialized. If your jurisdiction needs a specific listing, we tell you up front so you can plan for it.
Is there a warranty?
No factory warranty theater. What we back it with is real: parts stocked in LA, a serial-specific test record, and a filmed FAT. That's why the parts shelf matters.
How do prices work?
Posted on each model's spec sheet — no quote dance. Request the spec sheet for the model you want and the price and lead time are right there.
Why Pack Guys?
We're an LA operator, not a reshipper — we buy direct, badge and serialize in LA, film the acceptance test, and stock the wear parts here. Posted prices, a record on every unit, and a real person who watched yours run.
